Should I Post?

How it works

Select your platform, the region where most of your audience is, your post type, and your goal. The tool uses those inputs to find the best upcoming posting window.

Activity levels are scored at 15-minute intervals, weighted by region, and projected up to 12 hours ahead. If now is already a strong window, it tells you to post. If a better window is coming, it tells you roughly how long to wait.

The Timing setting controls the threshold. "Good enough" accepts a wider window and recommends posting sooner. "Best possible" holds out until activity is close to peak.

Everything runs in your browser. No personal data is used — the scoring is based on typical activity patterns for the platform and region you choose, not your own account analytics.
