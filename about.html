Should I Post?

About

Should I Post? is a free tool that helps you decide whether to post on social media now or wait for a better window.

It looks at the current time and compares it against generalized activity patterns for your chosen platform and audience region. The result tells you whether now is a strong window, whether a better one is coming soon, or whether you should hold off.
