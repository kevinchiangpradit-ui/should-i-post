FAQ

Is this based on my personal analytics?

No. The tool has no access to your account, followers, or analytics. It uses generalized activity patterns for the platform and region you select.

Does it work for multiple platforms?

Yes — Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn each have separate timing models tuned to their typical usage patterns.

Why does the recommendation change by post type or goal?

Different post types and goals tend to peak at different times. A Reel and a text post don't follow the same engagement curve, so the tool adjusts the scoring based on what you're posting and what you're trying to achieve.

Is this exact or just guidance?

It's guidance. The patterns are based on typical platform usage, not your specific audience. Treat it as a useful starting point, not a guarantee.

Can I still post outside the recommended window?

Yes. The tool gives you a signal, not a rule. Great content can perform well at almost any time. This just helps you avoid posting when almost no one is likely to see it.
