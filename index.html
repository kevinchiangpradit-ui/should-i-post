FAQ

How does this work?

It checks the current time against known activity patterns for your chosen platform and audience region, then tells you whether it's a good time to post or when to wait.

Is this using my account data?

No. The tool has no access to your account, followers, or analytics. It uses general usage patterns for the platform and region you select.

How accurate is this?

It's a general heuristic based on typical platform usage patterns — not your personal analytics. Treat it as a solid starting point, not a guarantee.

Does it work for all platforms?

Yes — Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn each have separate timing models tuned to their typical usage patterns.

Why isn't my platform listed?

This tool only covers platforms where post timing has a clear, short-term impact on reach. For others — like YouTube or Pinterest — timing matters less, or the patterns aren't consistent enough to be useful.

How it works

Select your platform and the region where most of your audience is. The tool scores activity levels hour by hour, weighted by region, and finds the best upcoming window within the next 12 hours.

No personal data is used. Everything runs in your browser using time and regional activity patterns.

The Timing setting lets you choose how close to peak you need to be — flexible accepts a wider window, strict waits for the best moment.

Privacy

No login required. No account access. No personal data collected or stored. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

This site may use basic anonymous analytics (page views) to understand traffic. No cookies are set for tracking.
